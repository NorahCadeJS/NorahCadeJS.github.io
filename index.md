## About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the Knight Foundation School of Computing and Information Sciences, Florida International University, advised by Prof. Xuyu Wang. My research focuses on **3D vision for wireless field modeling**, **Physical AI**, **digital twins**, and **world models**.

My work bridges machine learning, 3D vision, and wireless sensing by developing physically grounded representations for reconstructing and understanding wireless environments. I am particularly interested in neural rendering, 3D Gaussian Splatting, geometric algebra, and physics-aware wireless radiation field reconstruction.

I am actively seeking internship opportunities in frontier AI research, including 3D vision, Physical AI, wireless sensing, digital twins, and world models.
