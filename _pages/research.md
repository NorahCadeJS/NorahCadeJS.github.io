## Research Vision

My research aims to build physically grounded AI systems that can reconstruct, simulate, and reason about wireless environments. I study how 3D vision and neural scene representations can be extended from visual signals to wireless radiation fields, enabling digital twins and world models for sensing and communication systems.

## Research Areas

### 3D Vision for Wireless Fields
I develop 3D scene representations for wireless channel and radiation field modeling, including NeRF-style representations and 3D Gaussian Splatting frameworks adapted to wireless propagation.

### Physical AI
I am interested in AI models that obey or incorporate physical constraints, including wave-equation priors, physics-informed regularization, and scene-aware modeling of wireless propagation.

### Digital Twins and World Models
My long-term goal is to construct wireless digital twins and world models that support reconstruction, prediction, simulation, and active sensing in dynamic environments.

### Neural Rendering and Geometric Algebra
I study neural rendering pipelines, 3D Gaussian representations, and geometric algebra-inspired formulations for modeling spatial structure and wireless propagation.
